# IELTS Speaking 复习卡｜Travelling & Helping Others

> 学习目标：先独立说清楚，再提高流利度；目标为雅思口语 6.0+（平时按 6.5 的方向训练）。以下均为练习示范，不代表背出来即可获得指定分数。

## 怎样使用

- **第一轮：** 看中文提纲，用自己的英语回答。
- **第二轮：** 对照参考答案，检查时态、单复数、固定搭配。
- **第三轮：** 遮住英文稿，独立录音并回听。不要只跟读。
- **时间参考（练习目标，不是官方硬性时长）：** Part 1 每题约 15–25 秒；Part 2 连续约 1 分 40 秒至 2 分钟；Part 3 每题约 30–45 秒，必要时进一步展开。

## Part 1 — Travelling

### Q1. Do you often ride a bicycle?
**思路：** 不常骑 → 更常骑电动车 → 骑自行车累。

**示范答案：**
> No, I don't ride a bicycle very often. I usually ride an electric bike instead because cycling is quite tiring.

**注意：** often（不是 *oftenly*）；electric bike（不是 *electronic bike*）；tiring 描述事情令人疲劳，tired 描述人感觉疲劳。

### Q2. What transport do you often use when travelling?
**思路：** 公共交通 → 火车和公交车 → 方便且便宜。

**示范答案：**
> When I travel, I usually use public transport, such as buses and trains, because they're convenient and affordable.

**注意：** buses / trains 用复数；public transport（英国常见）或 public transportation（美式常见）都可以。也可以用 cheap，但 affordable 更自然地表达价格负担得起。

### Q3. Do you prefer to sit by the window when travelling?
**思路：** 喜欢靠窗 → 看风景 → 放松。

**示范答案：**
> Yes, I prefer to sit by the window because I can enjoy the views outside, which makes me feel relaxed.

**注意：** by **the** window；which makes 指前面“欣赏风景”这件事。也可以简化为：`It helps me relax.`

### Q4. Did you have a long journey with your family as a child?
**思路：** 小时候春节 → 和家人开车回老家 → 7–8小时 → 路上听歌聊天吃零食。

**示范答案：**
> Yes. When I was a child, my family used to drive back to our hometown for the Spring Festival. The journey usually took seven or eight hours. During the trip, we listened to music, chatted and ate snacks.

**注意：** used to + 动词原形，表示过去常常做；usually 和过去时能连用，`usually took` 没问题；`drive back to our hometown` 需要 **to**；`listen to music` 需要 **to**。

## Part 2 — Describe a time when you helped someone

**Cue card**
- Where you helped him/her
- Why you helped
- How you helped
- And explain how you felt about it

### 中文提纲
1. 我和两个室友去大学附近商场逛街。
2. 商场在马路对面，我们在人行道上等着过马路，突然下雨。
3. 路边有四个人：两个室友、陌生女生和我。
4. 室友共用一把伞，我有自己的伞；女生没伞，用手挡雨。
5. 我没想太多，主动靠近一点，把伞的大部分倾向她。
6. 绿灯后一起过马路，她道谢，然后各自离开。
7. 我觉得满足、开心；只是举手之劳，希望以后也能在合适情况下帮人。

### 英文示范（从真实经历改写，便于记忆）
> I'd like to talk about a time I helped a stranger near my university.
>
> One afternoon, I was walking to a mall with my two roommates. We wanted to go shopping. The mall was on the other side of the road, so we were standing on the pavement, waiting to cross the road.
>
> Suddenly, it started to rain. There were four people there: my two roommates, a girl I didn't know, and me. My roommates were sharing an umbrella, and I had my own. But the girl didn't have an umbrella. She was using her hands to cover her head.
>
> She was standing about half a step away from me. I didn't think too much about it. I just moved a little closer and tilted most of my umbrella towards her to keep her dry. She seemed a little surprised at first, but she stayed beside me.
>
> When the light turned green, we walked across the road together, sharing my umbrella. After we crossed the road, she thanked me, and we went our separate ways. I continued to the mall with my roommates.
>
> I felt really happy because I had done something good. It wasn't a big deal, and I didn't have any special reason for helping her. I just wanted to help. I think small acts of kindness can make a difference, and I'd be happy to do something similar whenever I can.

**口述提醒：** 这一版包含少量便于展开的衔接细节；不确定真实发生过的内容（例如她的表情）可以省略，不必编造。能否说满接近两分钟取决于个人语速、停顿及扩展程度。

### 关键表达
| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| on the pavement | 在路边人行道上（英式） |
| pedestrian crossing | 人行横道 / 斑马线 |
| on the other side of the road | 在马路对面 |
| wait to cross the road | 等着过马路 |
| the light turned green | 绿灯亮了 |
| share an umbrella | 共撑一把伞 |
| tilt my umbrella towards her | 把伞朝她那边倾斜 |
| keep her dry | 避免她被淋湿 |
| go our separate ways | 各自离开 |
| It wasn't a big deal. | 这不是什么大事 |
| I just wanted to help her. | 我当时就是想帮她一下 |
| a small act of kindness | 举手之劳，小小的善举 |

## Part 3 — Helping Others & Parenting

> Part 3 不只列观点。训练结构：**观点 → 原因 → 例子／结果**；必要时再加另一面或限制条件。

### Q1. Should people be very kind when they help others?
**提纲：** 应该友善 → 别人更愿接受 → 耐心说话让人自在。

> Yes, I think people should be kind when helping others because a friendly attitude makes it easier for others to accept help. For example, speaking patiently can make others feel more comfortable. I also think it's important to respect their feelings instead of making them feel embarrassed.

**易错：** a friendly attitude **makes**（第三人称单数）；`make others feel comfortable`。

### Q2. Should children be taught to be kind to others?
**提纲：** 应该 → 和同学建立好关系 → 学习尊重、理解别人。

> Yes, definitely. I think children should be taught to be kind to others from a young age. For example, being kind to their classmates can help them build good relationships. It also teaches them to understand other people's feelings.

**易错：** `build good relationships`（不是 `a good relationships`）。

### Q3. Should parents help their kids with their homework?
**提纲：** 遇到难题时应该帮 → 用简单方法解释 → 不要直接替孩子完成。

> Yes, I think parents should help their children with homework when they have trouble with difficult questions. Parents can explain the questions in a simpler way, which helps children understand the problems. However, parents shouldn't do all the homework for them, because children also need to learn to solve problems by themselves.

**易错：** `explain the questions in a simpler way`；`have trouble with`；`by themselves`。

### Q4. What kind of advice should parents give to their children?
**提纲：** 善待他人 → 建立关系 → 独立解决问题 → 必要时求助。

> I think parents should give their children advice about both studying and daily life. For example, they should teach children to be kind and helpful to others. They should also encourage children to solve problems by themselves and ask for help when necessary. I think this can help children become more independent.

**易错：** `teach children to be kind`；`ask for help`；`become more independent`。

## 高频错误速查

| 容易说错 | 推荐说法 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| I ride a bicycle oftenly. | I don't ride a bicycle very often. | oftenly 不是标准词 |
| an electronic bike | an electric bike | 表示电力驱动用 electric |
| cycling is tired | cycling is tiring | 事情令人累用 tiring |
| sit by window | sit by the window | 需要冠词 |
| it usually take / tooks | it usually took | 过去的习惯与耗时 |
| listened music | listened to music | listen 后接 to |
| a good relationships | good relationships | 复数不加 a |
| attitude make | attitude makes | 单数主语 |
| I have done something good（回忆当时感受） | I had done something good | 从过去时间点回看更早完成的动作 |
| she used hands cover head | she used her hands to cover her head | use ... to do ... |
